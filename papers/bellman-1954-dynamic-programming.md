# Some Applications of the Theory of Dynamic Programming—A Review

[Publisher record and abstract](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.2.3.275)

## Why this paper is in the graph

An early account by Bellman of the dynamic-programming branch underlying recursive value-based decision methods.

## Contribution and limits

The review develops general principles through deterministic and stochastic problems. It supplies historical grounding for breaking a sequential optimization problem into recursive subproblems.

The concept nodes use modern discounted-MDP notation. Their formulas and assumptions should not be treated as verbatim transcriptions of this review. This is also not the origin of Markov chains.

## Reading route

First understand immediate reward plus future value in the numerical Bellman example. Then use this review to connect that recursion with the broader optimization idea.
